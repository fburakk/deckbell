Deckbell privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

What stays on your device. Timer settings (length, position, style), keyed by the presentation's ID, and your license status are stored in your browser with chrome.storage.local.

What Deckbell reads. On docs.google.com/presentation pages it reads the page address, to know which presentation and slide is showing, and the size of the slide on screen. It does not collect, store or send the text, images or notes in your slides.

What is sent. Nothing, unless you enter a Pro license key. Then the key and our seller ID are sent to Polar (api.polar.sh) to check that it is valid: when you activate it, and about once a week afterwards. As with any web request, Polar sees your IP address. Purchases happen on Polar's site under Polar's privacy policy.

No analytics, ads or sale of data. Removing the extension deletes its stored data.
