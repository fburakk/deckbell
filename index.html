Deckbell

A timer on your Google Slides

Deckbell is a Chrome extension for teachers, trainers and speakers. Pick a length, start the slideshow, and the countdown appears on your slide. Drag it anywhere, click it to pause.

Good to know

  • Nothing is added to your file. The timer is drawn on your screen, so your audience sees it when you project or share your screen.
  • Your slides' text and images are not collected or sent. Read what is stored.
